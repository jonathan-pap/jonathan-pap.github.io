About BluPulse

Insights, tutorials, and patterns — blue to purple.

BluPulse is a personal writing space for notes on data modeling, analytics engineering, and the small craftsmanship details that make technical work feel good — DAX, Power BI, SQL patterns, and the occasional deep dive when a problem is worth it.

Posts here favor why over what: less reference material, more reasoning about trade-offs and the decisions behind a solution.

How this blog works

Static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Posts live in content/posts/ as Markdown and are registered in content/posts.json. No tracking, no cookies, no build step.
